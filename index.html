What the system does

A complete examination cycle, from registration to results, using only a web browser.

For students

  • Register once and log in with an email and password
  • See the examinations that are open to you
  • Read the instructions before you start
  • Move between questions freely and see which ones are answered
  • A countdown timer submits your answers automatically at zero
  • Get your score, percentage and pass or fail status at once
  • Review every examination you have taken

For administrators

  • Create examinations and open or close them
  • Add, edit and delete multiple-choice questions
  • View registered students
  • View results and filter them by examination
  • Review the monitoring events recorded during examinations
  • See totals and average scores on the dashboard

How an examination works

  1. RegisterCreate a student account and log in.
  2. ChoosePick an available examination on your dashboard.
  3. ReadCheck the duration, pass mark and rules.
  4. AnswerAnswer the questions before the timer ends.
  5. ResultSubmit and see your marked result immediately.

Basic Browser-Based Examination Monitoring

During an examination the page watches for tab switches, window changes, exiting fullscreen and restricted actions such as copying or right-clicking. Each event is recorded for the examiner, and three warnings submit the examination automatically.

This is simple browser monitoring, not AI proctoring. It cannot prove or prevent cheating on its own.
